Looking through a keyhole

It never sees the whole shape. It has to put one together.

The world

The whole shape. Nothing in here ever gets to see this — the box is the only part it is looking at right now.

Its eye

All it ever receives: a small window, moving. One glance covers under a third of the shape.

What it has pieced together

Every glance put back where it came from. Watch the shape appear out of the fragments.

Each shape is the same three blobs every time. Only their arrangement differs — so you cannot tell them apart by what the pieces look like, only by where they sit relative to each other. Two of the four share their distances from the centre exactly and differ only in which blob went where.

Every so often a shape appears at an angle that was NEVER SEEN during training. It is the same shape, just turned.
